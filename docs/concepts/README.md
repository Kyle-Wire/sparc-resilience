# Understanding SPARC — A Reader's Guide

Welcome. If you've ever looked at a map of your city and wondered *why* some neighborhoods are hotter, wetter, or more polluted than others — and more importantly, what could actually change that — you're in the right place.

SPARC is a platform that takes that question seriously. Not just "what's happening here?" but **"what caused it, and what would happen if we intervened?"** That's a harder question than it sounds, and getting it right requires bringing together ideas from physics, statistics, machine learning, and causal inference in ways that haven't really been done before at this scale.

These guides are written for people who are curious and want to understand what's actually going on — city planners, climate funders, scientists from adjacent fields, skeptical technologists, and anyone else who thinks the planet deserves better tools than a spreadsheet and a gut feeling.

You don't need a math background. You need patience and a willingness to let ideas build.

## A Note on What You Won't Find Here

These guides explain concepts freely. They don't explain how SPARC is built under the hood. The architecture, training procedures, and implementation details are proprietary. What you'll find here is the *why* — the intellectual lineage and the intuitions that make the outputs meaningful.

Think of it like understanding how a great chef's food works — you can taste the acid balancing the fat, feel the texture of the emulsion — without knowing the exact recipe.
